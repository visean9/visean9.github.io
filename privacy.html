Privacy Policy — Roberts Family HQ

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Roberts Family HQ is a private tool used only by the Roberts family. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the family's permission, it reads and writes the family's own Google Calendar, Google Sheets and Gmail (heyrobertsfamily@gmail.com) to show schedules, send reminders, keep to-do lists and save draft email replies. It never sends, deletes or pays anything on its own.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide these reminders and summaries to the family. Email content may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic) solely to summarise it. Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
